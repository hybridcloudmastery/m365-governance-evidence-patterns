# Audit Evidence

## The management question

Was relevant Copilot activity recorded during the assessed period?

Microsoft 365 audit evidence can demonstrate recorded activity. It does not provide a complete description of current configuration, historic behaviour or effective access.

## Evidence boundaries

A formal review should record:

- tenant and assessment date;
- authorized collecting identity;
- search period;
- applicable retention;
- search criteria and result limits;
- errors, incomplete results or unavailable detail.

## What a Copilot interaction establishes

It establishes that Microsoft recorded a Copilot interaction associated with an identity and time.

It does not automatically establish:

- paid Copilot licensing;
- organizational-data access;
- unauthorized disclosure;
- a privacy breach;
- the content of a prompt or response.

The detailed event context must be assessed before those conclusions are considered.

## Empty results

An empty result means:

> No matching event was returned within the documented search, permission and retention boundaries.

It does not mean that the activity never occurred. Activity may fall outside retention or the assessed period, while incomplete access or collection failure may also affect the result.

## Governance practice

Preserve the authorized evidence separately from the management summary. Treat the evidence as potentially sensitive because it may contain personal identifiers, timestamps, network information and resource references.

If the search reaches its result boundary, the population must be treated as incomplete until additional retrieval and reconciliation are performed.
